# Classroom Facilitation Script

## Format

- Recommended duration: 90-100 minutes
- Participants: one facilitator and four stakeholder groups
- Classroom format: three formal negotiation rounds plus a final resolution vote
- Digital demo: two rounds, followed by the central coordinator's final decision

## 0-10 Minutes: Setup

1. Introduce the nickel supply chain and the RKAB quota decision.
2. Explain that the exercise is a simulation, not a prediction.
3. Assign four stakeholder groups and one central coordinator.
4. Distribute:
   - `docs/background.md`
   - `docs/role-briefs.md`
   - `docs/agenda-template.md`

Facilitator instruction:

> State your interests and conditions, but do not invent evidence you do not have.

## 10-20 Minutes: Required Briefing

Review the causal chain:

```text
international structure -> domestic filtering -> strategic perception -> state capacity -> policy
```

Ask each group to identify one external opportunity, one external constraint, and one domestic cost.

## 20-40 Minutes: Round 1 - Initial Positions

1. The central coordinator presents the first quota proposal.
2. Each group gives an opening statement.
3. Each group identifies:
   - one acceptable element
   - one unacceptable element
   - one condition for supporting the package

Facilitator records all conditions in the resolution template.

## 40-60 Minutes: Round 2 - Conditional Bargaining

1. The central coordinator revises the quota pace or compensation measures.
2. Stakeholders may exchange support for specific safeguards.
3. Each group must distinguish:
   - policy support
   - willingness to carry out a task
   - conditions that remain unmet

No actor is assumed to consent because it remains silent.

## 60-75 Minutes: Round 3 - Implementation Review

Check:

- legal authority
- fiscal and administrative capacity
- necessary cooperation
- verification indicators
- unresolved community and environmental risks

The central coordinator chooses:

- adopt the resolution
- continue revising
- maintain the quota baseline

## 75-85 Minutes: Final Vote

Each group votes:

- support
- conditional support
- oppose

Conditional support counts only if the stated condition is included in the final text. The facilitator reads the final resolution and records dissenting views.

## 85-100 Minutes: Debrief

Ask:

1. How did international structure shape domestic bargaining?
2. Which actor had the strongest bargaining power, and why?
3. Did international pressure alone determine the outcome?
4. How were transition costs distributed?
5. Which commitments were credible, and which remained symbolic?
6. What would change if communities had stronger legal veto power?

## Facilitator Rules

- Do not reveal private role objectives directly.
- Do not treat stakeholder support as proof of implementation.
- Require evidence or explicit assumptions for quantitative claims.
- Record unresolved conflicts rather than forcing artificial consensus.
- Preserve the distinction between historical evidence and simulated behavior.
